About Palmer

Let's celebrate

Every meal is a celebration when it's shared around a beautifully laid table with those we love. Good food, family and friends are all we really need, but add just a twist of colour or a daring personal touch and you'll inspire experiences to remember.

Our Vision

There is no better place to celebrate life than together at a beautifully set dining table. Our vision is to inspire and elevate the dining experience worldwide, creating moments of joy and connection.

Our Mission

Palmer brings life to dining tables all over the world. Through our unique and high-quality designs, we help hosts and chefs create unforgettable dining experiences that ignite the senses.

Our Story

Founded as part of the Wegter family of tableware brands, Palmer has always been driven by a passion for design and a love for people. We craft our collections with care, ensuring each piece not only looks beautiful but also stands the test of time.

Our core values

We design dinnerware that brings people together and makes every table feel like a celebration.

Quality for longevity and ease of use

Our products are suitable for professional use and designed to be enjoyed for a long time.

For people and planet

Our products are produced from carefully selected sources and sustainable materials wherever possible.

The base for a unique experience

We let our creativity flow and draw inspiration from nature, cities, people, cultures, scents, and colors.

Original

All Palmer collections are our own designs, giving chefs and hosts a distinctive dining concept.
